## 💡 The Recruiter Snapshot: Why Me?

Most design pipelines suffer from a handoff disconnect: designers create unfeasible mockups, developers struggle to interpret design systems, and QA discovers user-flow bugs too late.

**My unique multi-disciplinary background bridges all three worlds:**

1. **User-Centered Product Mindset (Google UX Certificate):** Trained in end-to-end design thinking — from foundational user interviews and empathy mapping to rapid wireframing, high-fidelity Figma components, and usability studies.
2. **Quality Assurance Precision (Ubisoft QA Alum):** Evaluated complex user interactions, menus, and HUD logic under strict AAA software standards. I spot cognitive friction, broken edge cases, and accessibility blockers before they reach engineering.
3. **Front-End Feasibility (B.Tech IT):** Strong foundation in modern semantic HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and responsive design systems. My designs are built with implementation reality in mind — saving engineering cycles and eliminating design debt.

## 🚀 Flagship UX Case Studies

### 🍣 1. "Suzoki" — Food Customization App
*A user-first mobile dining app designed to eliminate menu fatigue and hidden topping costs.*

- **The Problem:** Budget-conscious diners face decision fatigue from cluttered multi-page menus and unexpected price spikes during dish ingredient customization.
- **The UX Solution:** Structured a streamlined ingredient customization sheet with real-time upfront price increments (`+¥100`), top category quick-switching pills, and a single-tap order flow reducing checkout time by 30%.
- **Methods & Deliverables:** User Interviews, Empathy Mapping, Persona (*Kodama*), User Journey Maps, 3 Paper Wireframe Iterations, Low-Fi & High-Fi Prototyping, 2 Rounds of Usability Studies.
- **Explore Project:**
  - 🖥️ **[Interactive 25-Slide Deck](https://kousic17.github.io/portfolio/deck-suzoki.html)** *(Includes full paper sketches, wireframes, and usability findings)*

### 💻 2. Teenager E-Learning Platform
*An interactive coding workshop platform tackling isolation and low completion rates in online teen education.*

- **The Problem:** 70% of teenagers find solo video courses disengaging and lack affordable access to in-person tech bootcamps.
- **The UX Solution:** Designed a collaborative dual-pane learning environment pairing video playback with synchronized peer chat and an instant 1-click community mentor help overlay.
- **Methods & Deliverables:** Demographic Surveys, Persona (*Darren Conner*), Information Architecture, Course Filter Grids, Student Progress Badging, Adobe XD Prototype.
- **Explore Project:**
  - 🖥️ **[Interactive 25-Slide Deck](https://kousic17.github.io/portfolio/deck-elearning.html)** *(Complete case study presentation)*
