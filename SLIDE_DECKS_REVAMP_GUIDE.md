### Slide 15: Usability Study Findings
- **Header:** `Usability study: findings`
- **Introduction (Filled in):**
  > "I conducted two rounds of moderated and unmoderated usability studies with 5 participants to evaluate ordering speed, category navigation, and ingredient customization clarity."
- **Round 1 Findings:**
  - `1`: To get an early insight into the app’s usability before the introduction of visual elements, I recruited 2 participants to test the low-fi prototype.
  - `2`: By watching participants interact with the app and hearing their thoughts, I was able to identify problem areas in the app's navigation. Results were synthesized using an affinity diagram.
  - `3 (Filled):` **Price Ambiguity:** Participants were uncertain whether toppings were included in the base meal price or incurred additional costs, leading to hesitation at checkout.
- **Round 2 Findings (Filled in):**
  - `1 (Filled):` **Faster Discovery:** Adding top category tabs allowed 100% of participants to locate their desired cuisine in under 10 seconds.
  - `2 (Filled):` **Cost Transparency:** Adding explicit (+¥100) tags next to each topping eliminated checkout surprise and boosted customization confidence.
  - `3 (Filled):` **Streamlined Ordering:** The revised ingredient checklist enabled participants to customize and place orders in under 60 seconds.
