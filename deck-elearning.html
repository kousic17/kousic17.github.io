Project overview

The product:
Online E-learning platform for teenagers holding a range of in-person courses and workshops at various locations.

Project duration:
3-week design exercise to build an engaging digital workshop solution for students.

Google

Project overview

The problem:

Many teenagers are unable to attend in-person workshop camps due to geographic distance and high cost, but still want to take part in hands-on coding courses without feeling isolated.

The goal:

Provide an engaging online learning environment with collaborative peer support. Measured by tracking weekly user logins and active student participation.

Responsibilities:

User research: summary

I conducted interviews with prospective students curious about learning technology and created empathy maps to understand learning barriers.

During research, it was discovered that 70% of participants were interested in interactive online workshops to get hands-on practice, rather than solitary video lectures.

Google

User research: pain points

1

Boring Content

Lectures feel dry and disengaging without interactive practice.

2

Unclear Guidance

Lack of clear explanations when students get stuck on coding errors.

3

Student Profiles

Desire for public student profile cards to showcase milestones.

4

Course Ratings

Need for peer reviews to evaluate which workshops are worth taking.

Usability study: findings

I conducted usability studies with teenage students to evaluate lesson engagement, video player clarity, and ease of seeking peer help.

Key Observations:

Self-motivation is the key to keeping teenager attention. Students need to be engaged by subjects they care about and be provided with peer collaboration tools to continue succeeding.

Quantitative Insight:

70% of survey participants confirmed they preferred hands-on interactive workshops. While students concentrate well alone, getting feedback from motivated peers drastically increased course completion.

Accessibility considerations

1. Screen Readers

Descriptive image labels and clean semantic DOM hierarchy throughout.

2. Large Font Scale

Comfortable font sizes for tablet reading without eye strain.

3. Closed Captions

Added closed captions for all video lessons to assist hard-of-hearing students.

Takeaways

Impact:

Testing with real teenage users revealed that peer encouragement is a critical catalyst for course retention and long-term interest in technical careers.

What I learned:

Interviewing younger demographics requires persistent, empathetic questioning to encourage students to open up about hidden frustrations.

Google

Next steps

1. Extended Usability Studies

Conduct further studies across diverse schools to evaluate long-term project submission workflows.

2. Preventing Featuritis

Focus on perfecting core video engagement and peer mentorship before introducing complex community add-ons.
